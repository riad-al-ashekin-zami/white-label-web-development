## What Is a White Label Website Builder?

A **white label website builder** is website-building software developed and maintained by one company but offered to end users under another company's branding.

Imagine that your agency is called Example Digital.

Instead of telling clients:

> "Log into Company X to edit your website."

you could potentially give them something like:

`builder.exampledigital.com`

The dashboard could display your logo, company name, support links and other branding instead of prominently presenting the underlying software provider.

The technology belongs to the platform provider, while your agency owns the customer relationship and brand experience.

This creates a model similar to many other white-label services.

The provider concentrates on developing and maintaining the underlying product. Your business concentrates on acquiring customers, delivering websites, providing support, bundling services and generating revenue.

That distinction is important because **white-label software is not the same thing as white-label website development**.

With software, you are primarily licensing or reselling a platform.

With [white label website development](https://whitelabelwebsitedevelopment.com/), an agency can outsource actual design and development work to another development team while continuing to manage the client relationship under its own brand.

The two models can also work together.

An agency might use a website builder for straightforward projects while outsourcing more sophisticated projects that require custom development.

# How Does a White Label Website Builder Work?

The exact workflow varies, but a typical setup looks like this:

1. Your agency subscribes to a white-label or reseller plan.
2. You configure your logo, brand name, domain and other settings.
3. You create client accounts or websites.
4. Your team or client builds the website using the platform.
5. The client interacts with a branded or partially branded environment.
6. You charge the client according to your own service model.
7. The underlying provider maintains the website-builder technology.

More sophisticated reseller systems can also provide APIs, automated account provisioning, billing integrations, hosting, custom email notifications and domain masking.

For example, Brizy says its Cloud White Label Reseller program can provide custom domains for dashboards and project previews, branded dashboards, customized email notifications and development APIs.

**Outbound resource:** [Brizy Cloud Reseller documentation](https://support.brizy.io/hc/en-us/articles/4402974639633-What-is-the-Brizy-Cloud-Reseller-program)

This means a white label builder can become much more than an internal productivity tool.

It can become part of the product your agency sells.

# White Label Website Builder Pricing: What Does It Really Cost?

Pricing can be deceptive if you compare only the monthly subscription.

**Platform subscription + per-site fee + hosting + ecommerce fees + AI usage + premium features + development + support + migration costs**

For example, Duda currently advertises its White Label plan starting at $149 per month.

## Calculate Cost Per Client

Suppose your total platform expense is $300 per month and you have 30 paying clients.

Your base software cost is:

**$300 ÷ 30 = $10 per client/month**

If each client pays $79 per month:

**$79 - $10 = $69**

That $69 is **not profit**.

You still need to account for:

- customer support
- payment processing
- sales
- marketing
- taxes
- design work
- development
- administration
- revisions

But the calculation demonstrates why website subscriptions can become attractive at scale.

# White Label Website Builder FAQs

## What is a white label website builder?

A white label website builder is website-building software that allows another business to present some or all of the platform under its own brand. Depending on the provider, this can include your logo, domain, dashboard, client login, emails and other customer-facing elements.

## What is the best white label website builder?

There is no universal best option. Duda is particularly strong for established agencies, Brizy is compelling for AI, WordPress and reseller use cases, Simvoly targets agencies building branded recurring-revenue platforms, and Site.pro is particularly relevant to hosting companies.

## What is the best white label website builder for agencies in 2026?

Agencies should shortlist platforms such as Duda, Brizy and Simvoly based on their specific delivery model. Agencies should compare white-label depth, client management, SEO, performance, pricing, APIs, AI functionality and migration options rather than choosing solely from feature counts.

## What is the best white label AI website builder?

Brizy is one notable option because it explicitly combines AI website generation with white-label and reseller functionality. Other website-building platforms also provide AI features, but agencies should distinguish between "a builder containing AI" and "an AI builder that can be delivered as a genuinely white-label client experience."

## What is a website builder reseller?

A website builder reseller sells access to website-building technology supplied by another provider. The reseller may control customer relationships, branding, pricing and support while the original provider operates the underlying software.

## Can I put my own logo on a white label website builder?

Usually, yes, but the amount of customization varies considerably. Some products offer basic logo replacement, while deeper white-label platforms can support custom domains, dashboards, email notifications, support URLs and additional interface branding.

## Can I use my own domain?

Many dedicated white-label platforms provide custom domain or domain-masking capabilities. Verify exactly which parts of the customer experience can use your domain before purchasing.

## Can clients log into the website builder under my brand?

Some platforms provide branded client login screens and dashboards. Duda, for example, specifically provides white-label client login and dashboard functionality.

## Can I set my own prices?

Reseller-oriented platforms frequently provide greater control over how services are packaged and sold. The exact billing and pricing capabilities vary by vendor, so verify current reseller terms.

## Is there a free white label website builder?

Fully featured commercial white-label platforms generally involve costs somewhere in the business model. Free trials, free tiers, revenue-share arrangements and open-source alternatives exist, but "free website builder" should not automatically be interpreted as "free commercial white-label reseller platform."

## What is a white label WordPress website builder?

It is typically a WordPress page-building or website-building system where the original builder's branding can be replaced or hidden in favor of the agency's branding. Brizy provides one example of this model.

## Can WordPress be white labeled?

WordPress is open-source software and can be extensively customized. Agencies can create branded admin experiences and combine WordPress with themes, plugins and visual builders. However, creating a complete reseller platform requires more work than installing WordPress itself.

## Do white label website builders include hosting?

Many SaaS builders include hosting as part of their service. Others offer different deployment models. Site.pro, for example, supports both cloud and on-premises configurations. Always check whether hosting is included, optional or mandatory.

## What is a white label website builder API?

A website builder API allows other software to interact programmatically with the platform. Depending on the provider, APIs can support tasks such as account provisioning, license management, site creation and integration with SaaS or hosting platforms.

## Do I need a website builder API?

A small agency manually creating websites may not need one. A hosting company, reseller or SaaS platform that needs automated provisioning is much more likely to benefit from API access.

## What is the difference between white label website development and a white label website builder?

A white label website builder is software. White label website development is a service where another development team produces websites or development work behind the scenes for your agency. Agencies can use either model or combine both.

## Can I resell websites built with a white label website builder?

Many white-label products are specifically designed for agencies and resellers, but licensing differs between providers. Read the platform's commercial and reseller terms before establishing your pricing model.

## Are white label website builders good for SEO?

They can be, provided the platform gives sufficient control over technical and on-page SEO and produces performant websites. Evaluate metadata, URLs, redirects, canonical tags, schema, sitemaps, heading structure, image optimization and Core Web Vitals before standardizing your agency around a platform.

## What should I look for in a white label website builder?

Prioritize branding control, custom domains, client management, permissions, responsive editing, SEO, performance, hosting, SSL, ecommerce, APIs, AI capabilities, support, pricing scalability and website portability.

## Is a white label website builder suitable for SaaS?

Potentially. SaaS companies can integrate website-building functionality instead of developing an entire editor internally. For serious SaaS implementations, API capabilities, SSO, scalability, data ownership, infrastructure and pricing at high user volumes become critical.

## Is a white label website builder better than custom development?

Neither is universally better. Builders are excellent for standardized websites and high-volume production. Custom development is better when unique functionality, architecture, integrations or source-code control are essential.
